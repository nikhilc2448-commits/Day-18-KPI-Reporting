# 📊 Day 18 – KPI Reporting

## 📝 Project Overview

This project focuses on converting customer and order data into meaningful business insights using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

The Capstone Customers and Orders datasets are cleaned, merged, and analyzed using Python and Pandas.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Calculate important business KPIs
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Measure customer retention
* Identify repeat customers
* Compare revenue across regions
* Compare revenue across product categories
* Perform cohort analysis
* Interpret KPI results for business decision-making

---

## 📌 KPIs Calculated

### 1. Total Revenue

Calculates the total sales generated by the business.

### 2. Average Order Value

Measures the average amount spent per order.

### 3. Repeat Customers

Counts customers who placed more than one order.

### 4. Customer Retention Rate

Measures the percentage of customers with repeat purchases.

### 5. Revenue by Region

Compares sales performance across different regions.

### 6. Revenue by Category

Compares revenue generated by different product categories.

---

## 👥 Cohort Analysis

Customers are grouped based on their **JoinDate month**.

The total sales generated by each customer cohort are then calculated to understand how different customer groups contribute to business revenue.

---

## 📈 Best KPI

**Customer Retention Rate** is identified as the strongest KPI because it shows repeat purchasing behavior and customer engagement.

A high retention rate can indicate customer loyalty, but the result should always be evaluated based on the available time period and dataset.

---

## 📉 Worst KPI

The region with the lowest revenue is identified as the weakest business performance area.

Possible reasons may include:

* Lower customer demand
* Fewer customers
* Lower order values
* Limited marketing performance

Further analysis is required before making a business decision.

---

## 🔍 Business Interpretation

### Misleading KPI Reporting

A KPI can be technically correct but still misleading.

For example, reporting a high retention percentage without explaining the calculation method or time period may create an unrealistic picture of customer loyalty.

### Bad News vs Noise

A decrease in a KPI is considered more serious when it continues consistently across multiple periods.

A small one-time change may simply be normal variation or noise.

## 💡 Key Learning

A good KPI is:

* Specific
* Measurable
* Relevant to business goals
* Connected to a business decision
* Actionable

A metric that looks impressive but does not support a meaningful decision can be considered a **vanity metric**.
